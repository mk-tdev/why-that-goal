## Category position

**Microsoft Foundry:** a substantive integration: all generated narratives pass through the deployed model, typed schemas, evidence selection, review and bounded revision. Managed identity keeps credentials on the server. Current and cached stories, measured baselines and fallback are visibly distinguished. Foundry Agent Service, native Foundry tracing/evaluation services and managed agent orchestration are not used or claimed.

**Multi-agent:** the clearest differentiation is a controlled false-save test: structural provenance checks accepted the draft shape, the real reviewer requested a semantic correction, and the revised goal story was rechecked. The review view presents this as a recorded test, alongside current live handoffs. It also states extra latency/token cost and the small sample. Custom coordination is described honestly; we do not claim framework support or organizer approval of a specific implementation.

**Enterprise:** publicly inspectable service telemetry, health probes, quotas, managed identity, CI, replay provenance and safe publication support an adoption story. The requested public inspection session deliberately requires no identity proof; it is not an enterprise access-control claim. A private rollout can disable demo access and configure Entra. Shared state/budgets, external alerting, human editorial checks, tenant isolation and load targets are explicit next steps.

**Azure cloud native:** Container Apps + scoped managed identity + registry + Foundry is real deployed integration. A single replica and process-local state do not demonstrate distributed resilience; do not pitch hosting alone as a cloud-native award claim.
