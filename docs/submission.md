## Pitch ready for the project description

**Why That Goal? — See the Play Behind the Finish**

A goal tells you what happened. Why That Goal? lets you rewind the buildup and inspect what mattered: progressive passes, receiving runs and space opening or closing at the destination.

Our original synthetic match feed drives a cinematic 3D stadium, measured evidence and an automatic recap. Click a statement to replay the exact supporting interval with an on-pitch trace and camera. Goals, saves and intercepted attacks stay distinct. Fans receive a concise story; analysts can inspect measurements and provenance, with English/Spanish output and player-follow views.

Microsoft Foundry powers three specialized roles: a match analyst selects grounded findings, a storyteller drafts the narrative, and an evidence reviewer audits every target against the full snapshot. A typed coordinator records shared state, fact lookups and handoffs. Review can request one targeted revision; unresolved errors preserve a labeled measured fallback. This is custom TypeScript coordination over the Responses API, not Microsoft Agent Framework.

The app is publicly deployed on Azure Container Apps with managed-identity inference. Quotas, bounded concurrency, schema/citation validation, health probes, sanitized audits and visible service telemetry make its behavior inspectable. The open demo is intentionally accessible to everyone; tenant-bound access and shared state belong to a private production rollout.

Recorded tests include 40 temporal/degraded-data checks, four reserved authored live Foundry outcomes approved at approximately 21 seconds p95, and a controlled false-save draft caught and repaired by the reviewer. These small synthetic tests are not independent accuracy, availability or commercial-impact certification. Full reports, reproducible code and limitations are public.

Record actual app interaction. Do not edit a pending/fallback workflow into a claimed live approval. If showing a cached approved result or recorded evaluation, keep its label visible. Avoid third-party match footage, copyrighted music and unlicensed logos. A 110-second plan leaves ten seconds below the required maximum.

## Private pilot adoption plan

Start with a broadcaster's synthetic sandbox and human editorial sign-off. Measure producer time from received event to accepted graphic, factual-error/rejection rates, cue accuracy and workflow latency across a separately labeled review set. Those are proposed pilot measures, not achieved business outcomes.

Before real multi-tenant use, enable private identity, tenant/data isolation, shared session/job/budget storage, persistent monitoring with alerts, defined retention and data rights, and the broadcaster's overlay integration. Rehearse provider outages and load against agreed targets. Keep human editorial approval for publication. The current single-process demo demonstrates the interaction and controls, not that rollout's completion.
